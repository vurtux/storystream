Frequently Asked Questions

What is StoryStream?

StoryStream is a premium, ad-free audiobook and podcast subscription service. From captivating stories to thought-provoking discussions, it offers a rich library of local and international content – all easily accessible on your smartphone.

Who can use StoryStream?

StoryStream is available exclusively to Vodacom customers in South Africa. This includes prepaid, postpaid, and hybrid users.

How do I access StoryStream?

Accessing the service is easy! Simply sign up for a subscription by choosing the plan that best suits your needs. Once subscribed, you'll gain full access to StoryStream’s complete library of audiobooks and podcasts.

How much does it cost to subscribe to StoryStream?

You can choose from three subscription plans, available under the 'Subscription Offers' section in the main menu:

  • Daily: R5.00, with the first day free.
  • Weekly: R25.00, with the first day free
  • Monthly: R80.00, with the first day free.

What is included in the subscription?

StoryStream offers:

  • A World of Stories, All in One Place: Enjoy a vast library of audiobooks and podcasts spanning fiction, business, self-development, romance, and more – curated from top local talent and leading global publishers.
  • Content That Speaks Your Language: Listen to captivating content in multiple languages, including English and a variety of South African languages, designed to reflect our culture and connect with every listener.
  • Seamless Listening: Access StoryStream through a simple, mobile-friendly web-portal. Browse, select, and play your favourite content with ease, anytime you like.
  • Your Audio, Anytime – Even Offline: Download your favourite audiobooks and podcasts to enjoy offline, wherever you are – no data or internet connection required.

Is there a free version of StoryStream?

No. StoryStream does not offer a free version. However, new subscribers will receive a 1-day free trial of the premium service to explore all features. After the free trial offer you will be charged your daily, weekly or monthly subscription fee.

Is StoryStream available 24/7?

Yes, the service is available 24/7, providing you with unlimited access to local and international audiobooks and podcasts across a wide range of genres. Whether you're commuting, relaxing at home, or winding down before bed, StoryStream is there to entertain and inspire you anytime, anywhere.

Do I need special equipment to access StoryStream?

No special equipment or software is needed to access StoryStream. You can access the service using any device with internet connection, such as a smartphone, tablet or laptop.

Can I listen to StoryStream content offline?

Yes! You can download Story Stream audiobooks and podcasts to listen offline, without needing an internet connection. You will be limited to five downloads per day.

How do I cancel my subscription?

If you want to cancel your subscription, dial *135*997# and select “View All” to view the services you are subscribed to. Select StoryStream and cancel the service. Once you’ve cancelled your subscription, you’ll lose access at the end of your daily, weekly or monthly billing cycle. We’ll miss you, but you can subscribe again any time.

Can I switch between the three subscription plans?

Yes, you can. However, you will need to cancel your current subscription first (following the steps above) before subscribing to the new plan.

When does my subscription end/expire?

Your subscription to StoryStream doesn't have an expiry date. Just make sure to pay your subscription fee to keep accessing our platform and its features.

How can I contact customer support if I have further questions or issues?

For any queries, please contact us at customerservice@mobibox.mobi. For billing queries, you can contact the Vodacom call centre at 082135.

Are there any additional costs to using StoryStream?

Yes, if you're not connected to wifi, standard data charges will apply.

Can I access StoryStream from more than one device?

Yes. While each user is allowed only one subscription, you can access StoryStream from multiple devices, such as your smartphone, tablet, or computer. You just need to log in.

Will I have access to StoryStream after cancelling my subscription?

After cancelling your subscription, you will still have access to the service until the end of your billing cycle (e.g., 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days, depending on your plan). Your access will remain active until the end of your subscription period.

Why can’t I access StoryStream?

To access StoryStream, you'll need an active subscription priced at either R5.00 per day, R25.00 per week or R80.00 per month. Subscriptions are exclusive to Vodacom customers only. Please refer to the "How do I access StoryStream?" section for details on signing up. Please make sure you have enough airtime balance to access the content.
